PROFILE

About
me

I am an Architectural BIM Technician with a background in architecture and an MSc in Building Information Modelling and Digital Transformation. My experience spans architectural design, BIM, Revit modelling, technical documentation and construction coordination, with a particular interest in using digital workflows to improve the way buildings are designed and delivered.

My current work involves developing and coordinating Revit-based workflows, producing technical drawing packages, building information standards and supporting design coordination from concept through to construction. I have experience working across architectural design and UK construction, including site and factory environments.

I am particularly interested in the intersection of architecture, BIM and digital design — not simply modelling buildings, but understanding how information, coordination and technology can support better design and more efficient delivery.
